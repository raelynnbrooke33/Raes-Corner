Welcome!

Welcome to my personal webpage! My name is Rae, and I am a student at Christopher Newport University studying Psychology and Criminology. I created this website as a place to share a little bit about myself, my interests, the things I enjoy, and some of the experiences that have helped shape who I am today. From reading and writing to crafting, coloring, and watching television shows, I have quite a few interests that give me a chance to be creative and take a break from everyday life. I also enjoy learning about people and understanding what makes them think, feel, and behave the way they do, which is a big part of what led me toward studying psychology and criminology.

Throughout this website, you can learn more about me, my education, my interests, my favorite things, and some of the experiences that have helped shape my goals. I wanted this page to feel less like a collection of random facts and more like a small introduction to who I am. There is a lot more to a person than a major, a list of hobbies, or a favorite television show, so this website also gives me an opportunity to talk about some of the places I have lived, my experiences in college, and the ways I have continued moving forward when life has not always gone according to plan.

About Me

My name is Rae, and I am currently a student at Christopher Newport University studying Psychology and Criminology. I chose these subjects because I have always been interested in people and the many different factors that influence the way we think and behave. One of the things that interests me most about psychology is learning about the human mind and understanding why people do the things they do. I also want to use what I learn to help people, which is an important reason why these subjects are meaningful to me. I like that Psychology and Criminology allow me to look at people and behavior from different perspectives while also giving me the opportunity to explore questions that I genuinely find fascinating. I am still figuring out exactly where these interests will eventually lead me, but I know that I want my education to help me understand people better and give me the tools to make a difference in the lives of others.

Family has also been an important part of my life. I have three younger sisters, which means that growing up has rarely been a completely quiet experience. My family has also lived in different places because of my dad's work. He was based in Texas from 2013 to 2017, and my family lived there with him from 2014 to 2015. In November 2015, we relocated back to Virginia so that we could be closer to family while my dad deployed to Afghanistan. Moving between different places gave me the opportunity to experience different environments and learn how to adapt when circumstances changed. Although moving can be difficult, those experiences became an important part of my childhood and gave me memories and experiences that I would not have had otherwise.

My college experience has also been a major part of my journey. I originally attended High Point University, where I began college with a different idea of what my future might look like. My time at HPU taught me a lot about independence, college life, and what I wanted from my education, even though my path there ultimately changed. College did not always go according to plan, and I experienced circumstances that forced me to reconsider what I wanted and what direction would be best for me. Rather than allowing those changes to convince me that I had failed, I learned that sometimes changing direction is part of moving forward. My time at HPU is still an important part of my college story because it helped me understand myself better and ultimately led me to where I am now.

One of the most difficult experiences I have faced was being involved in a serious car accident during my college years. The accident changed many aspects of my life and created challenges that I never expected to have to deal with at that point in my life. It affected me physically and emotionally, and there were times when simply getting through everyday responsibilities felt much more difficult than it had before. At the same time, the experience taught me something important about myself. I learned that perseverance does not always look like accomplishing something huge or overcoming every obstacle immediately. Sometimes perseverance means getting up, continuing forward, and refusing to give up even when progress feels incredibly slow. That experience became a difficult chapter of my life, but it did not become the end of my story.

Eventually, I made the decision to continue my education at Christopher Newport University. Starting over at a new school gave me an opportunity to take what I had learned from my previous experiences and use it to build something new. I enjoy the CNU campus, the people, my classes, and the location, and I am excited to continue developing academically and personally while I am here. My goal is to become the best person I can be while continuing to learn about the subjects that interest me. I am also considering law school as a possibility for the future. I do not have every part of my future figured out yet, but I have learned that I do not need to have everything planned perfectly in order to keep moving forward. What matters to me is continuing to grow, learning from my experiences, and building a future that I can be proud of.

My College Experience

My college experience has taken a path that I never could have completely predicted when I first started college. I originally attended High Point University, where I began my college education with one idea of what I wanted my future to look like. My time at HPU was an important learning experience, both academically and personally. I learned what it was like to be responsible for my own education, manage college-level expectations, and begin making decisions about my future. Although I eventually realized that HPU was not the right place for me to continue my education, I do not see that experience as wasted time. It was part of the process of figuring out what I wanted and what kind of environment would allow me to grow. Sometimes the path you originally choose is not the path you ultimately stay on, and learning when to make a change can be just as important as knowing when to keep going.

My experience at Christopher Newport University has given me a chance to start a new chapter. There are several things that I enjoy about CNU, including the campus, the people, my classes, and the location. I like being in an environment where I can focus on my education while also having the opportunity to meet new people and experience college life in a different way. My classes are especially important to me because I am studying subjects that genuinely interest me. Psychology allows me to learn more about the human mind and the factors that influence behavior, while Criminology gives me another way to explore behavior, society, and the circumstances surrounding crime. I enjoy being able to study topics that make me ask questions rather than simply memorize information.

One of my biggest goals for college is to become the best version of myself that I can be. I want to grow academically, but I also want to become more confident, independent, and prepared for whatever comes next. My experiences before coming to CNU have taught me that plans can change unexpectedly, and I have learned to keep moving forward even when things do not turn out the way I originally imagined. I am still exploring what I want my long-term career to look like, but law school is one possibility that interests me. Whether I eventually pursue that path or discover another career that fits me better, I want my education to prepare me to work with and help people. For me, college is not simply about earning a degree. It is about discovering what I am capable of, learning from the experiences I have along the way, and building a future that reflects the person I want to become.

My Interests

Outside of school, I have several hobbies that I enjoy in my free time. Reading, writing, crafting, coloring, and watching television shows are all very different activities, but they have something important in common for me. They give me a way to step away from everyday life for a while and focus on something that I enjoy. I tend to gravitate toward fiction when I read because I enjoy getting absorbed in a story and following characters through experiences that are completely different from my own. Writing is similar because I can take an idea that appears in my head and turn it into something completely new. Sometimes I have a specific idea that I want to develop, and other times I simply start writing and see where the idea takes me.

I also enjoy creative activities such as cross-stitch and coloring mandalas. Crafting gives me something physical and creative to focus on, while coloring allows me to slow down and concentrate on the details of whatever I am working on. I like that these hobbies do not require me to accomplish anything particularly important. I can simply sit down, choose something to work on, and enjoy the process. Sometimes I want to create something from scratch, while other times I would rather follow a pattern or design. Either way, having something creative to do gives me a chance to put everything else aside for a little while.

Watching television is another one of my favorite ways to relax, especially when I find a show that completely captures my attention. One of my favorite shows is The Last Ship, and one of the things I enjoy about it is that there is so much happening throughout the story. I enjoy the characters, the overall storyline, and the different elements that come together throughout the show. Whether I am reading a fictional story, writing something of my own, working on a craft, coloring a mandala, or watching a television series, these activities all give me a way to temporarily disconnect from everything else. They are a chance to escape into a story, a creative project, or another world for a little while and simply enjoy the moment.

My Favorites

Everyone has a few things that they can always come back to, whether those things are favorite colors, shows, hobbies, or animals. For me, many of my favorites are connected to the things I enjoy doing in my free time. My favorite color is purple, which also happens to fit pretty well with the overall theme of this website. I have always liked the different shades of purple, from soft lavender to much deeper and richer shades. When it comes to television, one of my favorite shows is The Last Ship. I enjoy the show as a whole rather than having just one specific favorite part of it. The story, characters, and everything happening throughout the series are what make it something I enjoy watching.

Animals are another easy category when it comes to my favorites. My favorite animals are dogs and horses, mostly because I think they are incredibly cute. I do not have one single favorite book because I enjoy discovering different stories rather than choosing just one. These favorites may seem like small details, but they are some of the things that make up the everyday interests and preferences that make me who I am.
